# MiHi Brand Style Guide

## Brand Direction

We're refreshing the brand with a modern, polished, and subtly expressive visual identity. This update introduces a refined direction built around a carefully curated color palette that balances deep neutrals with vibrant accents, creating a sophisticated look that's clean, contemporary, and aligned with a refined urban character.

**Brand Essence:** Elegant Urban

---

## Color Palette

A balanced blend of sleek neutrals with muted coral and aqua, giving a refined, professional, and city-chic look.

### Primary Colors

#### Black
- **HEX:** `#1F1F1F`
- **RGB:** 31, 31, 31
- **CMYK:** 72, 66, 65, 75
- **Usage:** Primary neutral, backgrounds, strong text

#### Coral
- **HEX:** `#FF4F4F`
- **RGB:** 255, 79, 79
- **CMYK:** 0, 84, 66, 0
- **Usage:** Vibrant accent, CTAs, highlights

#### Aqua
- **HEX:** `#18F1E1`
- **RGB:** 24, 241, 225
- **CMYK:** 55, 0, 24, 0
- **Usage:** Secondary accent, interactive elements, freshness

---

## Typography

### Header Font
**Azo Sans Uber**
- Use for: Headlines, titles, section headers
- Weight: Regular
- Character: Bold, commanding presence
-upper case letters

### Body Text Font
**Azo Sans Regular**
- Use for: Body copy, descriptions, long-form content
- Weight: Regular
- Character: Clean, highly readable

### Typography Guidelines
- Maintain clear hierarchy between headers and body text
- Ensure sufficient contrast for readability
- Use generous line spacing for body text
- Headers should command attention without overwhelming

## Design Principles

1. **Modern & Polished** - Maintain clean lines and contemporary aesthetics
2. **Balanced Sophistication** - Combine deep neutrals with vibrant accents thoughtfully
3. **Urban Character** - Reflect refined city-chic sensibilities
4. **Subtle Expression** - Allow color accents to punctuate without overwhelming
5. **Flexibility** - Design system accommodates future applications while maintaining cohesion

---

## Usage Notes

- This expanded palette offers flexibility for future applications while maintaining a cohesive, stylish visual identity
- The combination of deep neutrals and vibrant accents brings a balanced sense of sophistication
- Maintain the refined, professional character across all brand touchpoints
- Use coral and aqua as strategic accents against the primary black/charcoal foundation
