What is a 360 Photo Booth?

A 360 photo booth represents the next evolution in event entertainment and social media content creation. Unlike traditional photo booths that capture static images, a 360 photo booth uses a rotating camera arm that circles around guests, creating dynamic, slow-motion video content that looks straight out of a Hollywood production.

At MIHI Photo Booth, our 360 Photo Booth experience is built on three core elements:

  • Cinematic Motion – A high-speed rotating camera captures guests from every angle, creating smooth, professional-quality videos that feel like a red carpet moment.

  • Instant Social Sharing – Videos are processed on-site and delivered instantly to guests via text or email, ready to share across Instagram, TikTok, and other platforms.

  • Complete Customization – From branded overlays and music selections to themed backdrops and lighting effects, every element can be tailored to match your event's aesthetic.

The 360 photo booth has become the must-have entertainment choice for weddings, corporate events, bar and bat mitzvahs, proms, and high-profile celebrations throughout Boston and beyond.
