WHAT IS A
360 VIDEO BOOTH?

A 360 video booth represents the next evolution in event entertainment and social media content creation. Unlike traditional photo booths that capture static images, a 360 video booth uses a rotating camera arm that circles around guests, creating dynamic, slow-motion video content that looks straight out of a Hollywood production.

The 360 video booth has become the must-have entertainment choice for weddings, corporate events, bar and bat mitzvahs, proms, and high-profile celebrations throughout Boston and beyond.

INSTANT SOCIAL SHARING

Videos are processed on-site and delivered instantly to guests via text or email, ready to share across Instagram, TikTok, and other platforms.
