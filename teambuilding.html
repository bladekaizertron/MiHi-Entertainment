Important Information for Planning Your Program

Booking Window & Availability

We recommend securing your date 6-10 weeks ahead, particularly for fall programs (September through November) when corporate events peak. We maintain some flexibility for shorter-notice requests when our calendar allows.

Program Duration Options

Team building programs can range from 90-minute energizers to full-day experiences or multi-day retreats. We'll help you determine the right duration based on your objectives and schedule constraints.

Mountain Weather Preparation

Colorado mountain weather changes rapidly, especially at elevation. For outdoor programs, we always develop contingency plans and work with venues that offer indoor alternatives. Our team monitors conditions and makes real-time adjustments when needed.

Pre-Program Planning Process

After booking, we conduct a detailed planning session to understand your team's specific situation, desired outcomes, and any sensitivities to consider. This discovery process ensures the program delivers maximum value.

Participant Communication

We provide customizable communication templates for your team, including logistics information, dress code, what to bring, and program overview. Clear pre-event communication helps participants arrive prepared and engaged.

High Altitude Considerations

Programs at mountain elevations (8,000+ feet) require special planning. We adjust activity intensity, incorporate adequate breaks, and provide guidance on hydration and acclimatization for teams traveling from lower elevations.

Accessibility & Inclusion

We design programs that allow participation across different physical ability levels. During planning, we'll discuss any accessibility needs and ensure activities can be modified without compromising the team building value.

Documentation & Follow-Through

Professional photography/videography can be added to capture your team building experience. We also provide post-program resources and recommendations for reinforcing key learnings back in the workplace.
