Walk into History and Sophistication

 

Are you looking for an event venue that not only has sophisticated Aire to it, but also hold a little history? Well, you are looking in the right spot. While The Whittemore House began its history as a home rather than a venue, over the years it was frequented by many political figures. Afterall not many places have had a first lady give radio addresses from it. This stunning event venue will take you back in time in serious style.

 

Your DC photo booth will look gorgeous in the venue, its vintage look fitting right into its surroundings. The backdrops available are abundant, so you will, without a doubt find the perfect spot to make additional memories. Your wedding album, or even your roaring 20’s party album will be filled with amazing, fun-loving photos.

 

A Glimpse at What is in Store

 

The Whittemore House offers multiple event spaces for your elegant wedding or your lavish Gatsby party. (Yes, you can even see the library, that Eleanor Roosevelt made famous). From small to large, everyone will be comfortable. A room fitting for a smaller scale event, that has a stunning décor complete with a unique bay area, and a gorgeous pink marble hearth. To the largest event space that has an entry to a courtyard garden, dance floor and a beautiful fireplace.

 

Now, imagine how beautiful your photos will be! Step right up and strike a pose for your DC photo booth. Whether you would like to have a natural setting in the garden, or the stunning fireplace in the background, each photo will be perfectly matched to your vision.
