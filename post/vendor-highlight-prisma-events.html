When you think of Colorado weddings, you might imagine rolling foothills behind a gorgeous outdoor ceremony, or a rustic lodge-style venue surrounded by lush greenery and flowers. The food is superb, the rooms look glamorous and your guests are dancing the night away. As you dream of what your big day could be, there’s a team out there scouting venues, growing their design skills, and innovating their planning process so you can have exactly what you want from day one.

 
 
 

We love working with Prisma Events for just that reason. It’s one thing to say you have the skills to plan and coordinate a wedding, but what we value most in this business is their dedication to expanding their industry knowledge and connections so they offer the best- every time.

 
 
 

They’re not your cookie-cutter wedding planning team. Prisma Events prides themselves on being an inclusive planning and design firm that gives the utmost respect to what their clients want. They have one goal in mind for your big day, and it’s that your love story should be the center of attention, always. They want you and your boo to have fun during the planning process, be present during the entire day, and when you’re ready to ride off into the sunset- they’ll pack it all up as you go so you have nothing to worry about. That’s the mark of a group that truly cares about how you feel, before and after the event.

Planning and designing Colorado weddings since 2009, Owner Aimee Palifroni has a rich background in hospitality ranging from hotels, venues, catering, and restaurants, to make sure you have all the knowledge and support to make those big decisions for your big day. They think clients should be able to enjoy their planning process without having to concentrate on every single small detail. So let Aimee and her team handle the nitty gritty so you take some time to relax with friends and family.

 

And as you relax, you have more time to think of brilliant additions to your wedding, and trust that Prisma Events can get the job done. Dreaming of a fancy escort card champagne wall? They can do that! Want to reminisce with the most stunning photos of your day? They make sure to work with the best photographers around. How about wowing your guests with a surprise Drag Queen Show? Yeah, they know ’em! Whatever it is you want to bring out your style, personalities, and love story, they are there to make that happen. And the best part is that they love doing it! It’s their passion, and that will show in how enthusiastically they listen to your ideas.

 
 
 

Now, what sets them apart from the other companies? What kind of clients do they love working with? It’s couples who want to have a good time. Like, a really good time. They want the wedding planning process to be fun for everyone the whole way through. Their clients tend to be a lot like them – Type A with a side of sarcasm, open-mindedness and a strong work ethic. They create mid-luxury weddings for all kinds of couples, and are ready to begin planning your dream right alongside you. Give them a call or check out their website if you’re ready to have a blast!
