Ready to take a Closer Look

 

The one-acre botanical garden sits inside the atrium made of glass. This 6-story venue holds 50-foot arched ceilings, and is home to roughly 80 palm trees, that is right, palm trees in Chicago. The Crystal Gardens also has lush greenery, dancing fountains and twinkle lights.

 

Just imagine how lovely your wedding photos will be with the area as your background. From stunning flowers, to flowing fountains, or maybe the view of outside will without a doubt add something magical to the experience of the Chicago photo booth.

 

 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 

Some or All- It’s up to You

 
 
 

Depending on your style, guest list, or other preferences The Crystal Gardens will take good care of you. With options of using the full garden or 2/3 of it, you will be able to fit all your guests comfortably. Encompassing a dance floor and stage the full garden accommodates up to 600 guests for a seated dinner. If you prefer a cocktail style reception, you can share it with up to 1500 guests! When your event takes place in 2/3 of the garden, the area that will be sectioned off for privacy will look like you are surrounded by black trellising. Offering the perfect event space for smaller or moderate attendance numbers.

 

With shimmering lights, surrounded by your closest family and friends, hearts filled with love and joy. What better time or place to capture fond memories with your Chicago photo booth.
