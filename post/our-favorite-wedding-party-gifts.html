You picked your closest friends, most trusted companions and the ones you deem wedding-help worthy to stand next to you on your most special day as your groomsmen and bridesmaids. By accepting your wedding party invitation, they accept the titles of bathroom break dress holders, ring bearer babysitters and tissue producers. Why not give these amazing people the best “thank you” gifts possible? Need help finding something they’ll cherish long after you say “I do”? Below I’ve listed my top four favorite gifts for bridesmaids and groomsmen.

 

I’ve chosen all of these gifts solely because they have a good chance to be used outside of your wedding festivities. While totes, shot and wine glasses, and mini shooters that proudly display your wedding date are sure to inspire memories, I feel that bridesmaid and groomsmen gifts that are left without those details are usually worn, used or displayed more often.

 

Enjoy!

 

Bridesmaid Gifts

While running errands the other day, I told a fellow shopper that I loved her bracelet. Her response was “Thanks! I got it as a bridesmaid gift. Everyone loves it!”. I love that that woman kept wearing that heartfelt gift even after the wedding festivities, and I also loved that it was a piece of jewelry that easily matched any outfit.
