A Stellar Showcase Recap:

 

On March 12th, 2024, the Infinity Park Event Center transformed into a celestial wonderland for MiHi Entertainment's annual showcase event. With the theme "Googie Space Adventure," attendees were whisked away to a mansion on the moon, where the stars aligned for an unforgettable experience.

 
 
 
 
 
 

The Activations:

 

From the moment guests entered, they were immersed in a world of cosmic creativity. The Googie 360 Booth and Smoke & Lasers 360 Booth offered futuristic photo experiences, while AI Booths generated personalized badges and transformed individuals into famous moon-dwellers. The Eclectic Entourage Roamer captured candid moments, while vogue booths like Moon Crash, BioSphere, and Solar Flair provided Instagram-worthy backdrops. Adding to the ambiance was a roving robot photographer, capturing the event from a futuristic perspective. The interactive QR Passport allowed attendees to earn points and compete for bragging rights as they

 

explored each activation.

A Night to Remember:

 

Attendees hailed the event as the "Party of the Year," praising its immersive branding, on-theme decor, and interactive elements. From snapping selfies in space-themed photo booths to stargazing through telescopes, guests were dazzled by the creativity and innovation on display.
