A Southern Venue and Mississippi photo booth Waits for You

 
 
 

When your planning begins all the details you always though now come into play. Décor, setting, style, photos, are just a few of them. Take a look at The Antler, and find a little inspiration. Yes, this beautiful venue is nestled on a farm of over 50 acres, and offers its beauty for your special event such as family get together, roaring 20’s party, or your dream wedding. From the moment you first see the venue you will be inspired and intrigued by what it has to offer.

 

Throw in your Mississippi photo booth and see just how magical the results can be. Each photo capturing the perfect setting, backdrop and event memory with ease and enjoyment. The results will be seen right away when you receive them at the end of the night. Oh, yes, no more waiting for your photos, they are printed on site for you.

 

Inside, Outside, it is Up to You

 

The options at The Antler make it easy for you to find the perfect event space. If you are planning something elegant or more eclectic in style your event will be easily accommodated. Spaces that offer the modern conveniences while being easily customizable for your event. One space offering a classic ballroom feel, complete with stunning wrought iron chandeliers, a great place to dance the night away. Taking a step out of the gorgeous French doors will take you to the lovely courtyard and make it easy to take in the natural southern charm. The other space available sports amazing carriage doors, and antique ceiling tiles are just a few of the special details waiting for you.

 

Any space you choose will welcome your Mississippi photo booth as if it was meant to be. Each backdrop option offering a different style and touch to your final result. The moments of fun and joy to romantic and intimate all just a click and a print away.

The staff for your Mississippi photo booth will also welcome you and your event with open arms. Not just during the planning, but during your event as well. That’s right, you attendant will be there throughout the night to lend a helping hand for all of your photo needs.
