CSI DMC was founded by Jill McGregor-Hainline in 1987 in Washington DC. This woman-owned company describes its mantra as “always putting the client first”. Due to this objective, CSI became one of the most well-respected DMCs in the event industry and has grown to many regions of the United States and beyond, providing clients with exceptional experiences across the world!

 

CSI DMC Destination and Event Management has an amazing team of dedicated event professionals who seek to provide creative solutions for client events, virtual gatherings, memorable tours, engaging entertainment, rewarding incentive programs, seamless transportation, and experienced meeting management services. CSI DMC recognizes the fact that innovation is at the core of their company. The award-winning team produces fresh ideas, creative solutions, logistical expertise and has a deep local knowledge backed by extraordinary leadership that truly believes in ingenuity.

 

The award-winning team is consistently ranked as a Top 25 DMC. Their local team on the ground in Denver is backed by a national presence and top-tier partners. CSI DMC strives to consistently produce extraordinary, one-of-a-kind programming, serving as a one-stop resource for their clients, providing individualized service and attention to detail. CSI is dedicated to being your strategic partner and a true extension of your team, both in the greater Denver area, throughout the west, and in regions supported by their eleven strategic locations throughout the United States.

The team at CSI also has a strong belief that when “you do good, you feel good”. CSI is passionate about engaging with their local communities and giving back to their destinations is an integral part of their company culture. Both as individuals and together in their teams, they dedicate time and energy to the causes and organizations that are dear to their hearts. These causes include providing gifts to those in need, thanking our country’s heroes, cleaning up their destination cities, and “Keeping the City Warm” by providing winter clothing to many underprivileged citizens.

 

CSI DMC provides clients with special events, virtual experiences, experiential activities, transportation, meeting management, and dining experiences. In addition, they set themselves apart by giving back to the communities in which they serve. By tying the goals of your program to the authenticity of the destination. No matter the size or scope of your event, they are always excited to start planning. By learning more about CSI, you will learn they have a strong track record of success and excellence and will always be able to suit your needs and plan the perfect event.
