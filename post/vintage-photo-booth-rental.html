Vintage Photo Booth Rental at Bliss Bridal Boutiques

 
 
 

I love how many different style weddings there are in Colorado. We have been getting a lot of vintage prop theme requests for weddings coming up this fall and I found a gem recently. This is going to be an awesome vintage photo booth. When I was in New Hampshire visiting my family, I stumbled across this beautiful Rembrandt camera at an antiques store. My plan is to gut it and put a DSLR camera in it and refurbish the original components making it look like it is brand new. After I clean up the metal and sand/polish the wood I’ll have to fix part of the accordion and reinforce the tripod ensuring it can hold the new weight. My biggest concern was that there is no spot for a screen for guests to see themselves during their sessions, but I think we will add an addition below the camera which will hold a small screen.

 

Combined with the new camera and a high quality studio flash, this vintage photo booth will be a great addition to vintage style themed weddings. I will also offer a specific prop box that includes flapper girl accessories, top hats, paper fans, and of course, mustaches. Along with the props and newly refurbished camera, we will offer a vintage memory book and an optional sepia photo filter to keep each session looking true to the 20’s theme. Lastly, Jules will be able to design a unique photo strip for each event.

 

This will be our third photo booth in Denver, with the others being our Retro Booth and a modern photo booth that can elegantly fit in any event. Interested in reserving this booth for your wedding or theme party? Shoot us an email at info@mihiphotobooth.com or Contact us here
