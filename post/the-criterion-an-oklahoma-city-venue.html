EXPERIENCE HISTORY AT IT'S FINEST:

The Criterion is a historic event space built in 1921, restored to its original grandeur, and a sought-after venue for weddings, corporate events, and concerts in Oklahoma City. With architectural details like exposed brick walls, high ceilings, and a mezzanine level, this venue offers a unique and elegant setting for your special occasion. The venue can accommodate up to 4,000 guests, making it perfect for large-scale events.
