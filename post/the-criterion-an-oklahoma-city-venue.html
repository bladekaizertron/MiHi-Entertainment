EXPERIENCE HISTORY AT IT'S FINEST:

 

Looking for a venue with a rich history and charm that can make your special event truly memorable? Look no further than The Criterion in Oklahoma City. Built in 1921, this historic event space has been restored to its original grandeur and is now one of the most sought-after venues for weddings, corporate events, and concerts in the city. The Criterion boasts stunning architectural details, including exposed brick walls, high ceilings, and an elegant mezzanine level. And with a capacity of up to 4,000 guests, you'll have plenty of space to make your event unforgettable. Whether you're looking for a natural setting or the stunning décor of the venue, The Criterion provides endless possibilities for your next Oklahoma City Event, including beautiful photo booth rental opportunities that will capture every special moment.

 
 
 

THE CRITERION - WHERE THE GEMS OF OKLAHOMA CITY GATHER:

 

The Criterion is a venue that's as unique as it is historic. Originally built in 1921 and restored to its original grandeur, it boasts stunning Spanish and Mediterranean Revival-style architecture and a range of elegant event spaces. Whether you're hosting a wedding, corporate event, or concert, The Criterion is a venue that stands out in every photo and in the memories of every guest. With a large main hall, mezzanine level, and outdoor patio, there are plenty of ways to place your Oklahoma City Photo Booth Rental for guests to visit all night long. And with its capacity of up to 4,000 guests, you can be sure that your event will be unforgettable.

 

 

WHAT YOU'LL GET WITH THE CRITERION:

 

Get ready for an unforgettable event at The Criterion! This historic venue boasts three separate event spaces, each with its own seating, dancing areas, and spaces for food and gift tables. The Century Theatre space, where movies were once screened, is particularly picturesque and perfect for mingling or setting up your Oklahoma City photo booth. All three spaces can seat hundreds of people, and the stunning second balcony offers a bird's eye view of the main hall. If that isn't enough, The Criterion also offers exceptional food and bar service, as well as top-notch staff and event coordination. Don't believe us? Check out their rave reviews and take a look at their event packets to learn more about the venue's fascinating history.
