FEEL FAMOUS 2023: A Night to Remember

Administrator
•
December 12, 2025

On March 15th, 2023, our Feel Famous 2023 event took place at Sports Castle, an iconic venue in Denver, Colorado, owned by Non-Plus Ultra. The event was a huge success, with guests enjoying various activations across 6 floors that painted a Journey of Fame. One of the most popular activations was the 360 Booths, where guests could take 360-degree photos with fun props and backgrounds. The Smoke & Lasers Video booth was also a hit, providing a futuristic and immersive experience. The Cover Shoot Activation allowed guests to feel like they were on a magazine cover, while Aurora Sydel's live tattooing added a unique and artistic touch to the event.

 
 
 
 

The Light Painting activation was a crowd favorite, creating magical long-exposure photos with waving lights. The DJs, Interaction Denver and Dancin' DJ DJ & Lighting, provided an energetic and dance-worthy atmosphere, and guests enjoyed the open bars and delicious food and appetizers provided by House and Howell Social and Biscuits and Berries. Every guest also got to walk the red carpet to take a photo, and we created a personalized name badge for each attendee.

 
 
 

The event was made possible with the help of our amazing sponsors. All Digital Photo & Video captured stunning media, while Bear Aesthetic decorated and brought the vision together. Interaction Denver provided pipe & drape and top-notch DJ services, while Dancin' DJ DJ & Lighting also provided exceptional DJ services and lighting. Event Rents provided furniture and some pipe & drape, while Colorado Lighting and Event Services provided lighting and lit up the bar on the first floor.

 
 
 

Peak Beverage provided amazing bar service, and Scentex provided beautiful scents that added to the overall atmosphere of the event. Ignite Experiences provided a fun Tic-Tac-Toe board and their new Lego Wall installation where guests were able to fill the Feel Famous logo with legos throughout the night. Morgan Devillier provided amazing paintings for the 6th floor, and Occasionally Fabulous was our event planner who helped make our vision for the event come true.

 
 
 
 

We are grateful for the support of all our sponsors and everyone who attended Feel Famous 2023. It was truly a night to remember, filled with fun, laughter, and unforgettable memories.
