Add the Bay to Your Big Day

 

Beautiful Narragansett Bay, sparkling water, wonderful coast line, the sweet warm breeze. Doesn’t that sound like a fabulous place to start your new lives together. Belle Mer sits right there waiting for you. 7 wonderful acres lend themselves to multiple event spaces, all holding stunning views for you and your guests.

 

Did you say marry? Sounds like a great idea. Marry up your Newport photo booth with this great venue and see what magic can happen. The great part being that at the end of the night, you and your guests will be taking your photo memories with you.

 
 
 

Beauty around every Corner

 

The beauty of Belle Mer itself, and its surroundings is amazing. Imagine walking down a pathway made of crushed oyster shells, or in a beautiful garden. If that sounds like your style, you found the right place. That’s right, the island house is waiting for you. Not only with the garden, but wonderful fountains, a fireplace for a cool night, and being able to share it with close to 300 of your closest friends and family.

 

Roses, a fountain, or even the Newport bridge can be your Newport photo booth backdrop. No matter what your style, your photos will be better than you imagined. There is no doubt that your perfect spot will be found.

 

 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 
 
 

PRefer Inside while Looking Out?

 
 
 

The stunning event space known as the water salon at Belle Mer gives the inside luxury, while you take in the view through the glass wall. Special touches such as lighting that you can have change to match yours, and not just 1 but 2 fireplaces, add to the atmosphere and magic. If you want to incorporate both, the glass wall can be opened to use the lawn as well.

 

There are also many options here for your Newport photo booth. If you’re not exactly sure, ask the helpful staff, they can help with your idea and make sure your photos are just the way you want them. If you are planning your special day in the months where the nights get cooler, you can still have the beautiful outdoor background you want.

 
 
 

SUn, Water and Ceremony Circle

 

Belle Mer has a wonderful ocean lawn. Take in the impressive views of the bay, while enjoying the warm sun. The elegant ceremony circle with stunning walkways make your I do moments a little more magical. The other good thing is if you want to feel the breeze and smell the ocean with some shade, the space can be tented.

 

Each photo in your wedding album has its own story to tell. The people in them, their connection to you, the background, all combined for a perfect moment in time. Your Newport photo booth, will be capturing each one with ease. Your reception is your celebration, so why not add some fun in a unique way. Your guests will love your choice.
