We’ve had the pleasure of bringing our photo booths up to Boulder on many occasions. I love Boulder, and Boulder residents have proven their love for our photo booths by asking us to keep coming back again and again. That’s why I wanted to take a second to shout out my top four favorite Boulder venues that we’ve have the pleasure of working with.

 

 

Back in 2015 we were hired to provide our photo booth rental fun for a holiday at The Riverside. I loved the layout of this venue. It really flowed and the space let the crown mingle together with ease. Whether the guests needed a bathroom break or wanted to grab a drink from the bar, everything was perfectly placed making sure nothing felt crowded. I think this venue is perfect for those small company gatherings or a party with your closest friends.

 
 
 

Rembrandt Yard is a unique and beautiful wedding venue located in downtown Boulder, Colorado. It is an art gallery and event space that offers a modern and elegant atmosphere for weddings and other special occasions. One of the most notable features of Rembrandt Yard is its beautiful art gallery, which provides a stunning backdrop for wedding ceremonies and receptions. The gallery features high ceilings, large windows, and an open floor plan that can accommodate up to 300 guests. The space is decorated with contemporary art, providing a stylish and sophisticated ambiance that is perfect for a modern wedding. We've provided photo booth rentals for quite a few events at Rembrandt Yard, and they're all so beautiful.

 

 

Our favorite party planner Ricki Booker brought us onto her team of vendors for a bar mitzvah at the St. Julien Hotel. The layout of the area we were located was perfect for a diverse party agenda. Our Boulder photo booth rental and the cocktail bar was located outside of the ballroom where we provided early entertainment to the guests until the dinner and dancing began inside the ballroom.

 

 

Hotel Boulderado is a historic and elegant hotel located in downtown Boulder, Colorado. Built in 1909, the hotel has a charming Victorian ambiance and offers a variety of amenities and services for guests, including a restaurant, a spa, and event spaces. One of the most notable features of Hotel Boulderado is its stunning ballroom, which is a popular venue for weddings and other special events. The ballroom features high ceilings, chandeliers, and beautiful architectural details, providing a sophisticated and memorable backdrop for any event. For couples looking to capture memories from their special day, there are many local vendors in the area that provide Boulder photo booth rental services. With the elegant backdrop of Hotel Boulderado, the photo booth rental will offer a perfect addition to a wedding or other special event, providing guests with fun and memorable photos that will be cherished for years to come. Whether guests are staying at Hotel Boulderado for a special event or a vacation, the combination of the hotel's historic charm and modern amenities, along with the fun and unique addition of a photo booth rental, will create a memorable and enjoyable experience for all.

We’ve been able to meet some really amazing event professionals in Boulder and the venues we’ve been at have all had us inspired by their uniqueness. Here’s to 2023 bringing MiHi Entertainment the chance to provide the best Boulder photo booth rentals!
