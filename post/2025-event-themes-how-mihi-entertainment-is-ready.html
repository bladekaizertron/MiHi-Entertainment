Top Trending Event Themes for 2025

Imagine a modern twist on the classic cowboy aesthetic. This activation blends the rugged Wild West with eye-catching neon and electric hues. We reinvented our western saloon door setup, making it the perfect interactive place for photos.

Transport guests into a high-tech Western world with our AI-powered booth. Using advanced imaging and smart filters, attendees can see themselves transformed into a digital cowboy or cowgirl.
