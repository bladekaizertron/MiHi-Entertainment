Top Trade Show Venues in Las Vegas

Administrator
•
December 12, 2025

Looking to host a trade show in Las Vegas? The city’s dynamic energy and exceptional venues make it one of the most popular destinations in the U.S. for trade shows and conventions. To help you plan your event, we’ve curated a list of the top venues in Las Vegas, detailing their standout features and why they’re ideal for your next trade show.

 
 
 

1. Caesars Forum

 

  • Square Footage: 300,000 sq. ft.

  • Highlights: This state-of-the-art conference center is connected to several Caesars Entertainment properties, offering seamless access to accommodations and dining. Its flexible spaces are perfect for high-profile trade shows and corporate events.

  • Fun Element: Host a Roman-themed networking session with live performers dressed as centurions to create a memorable experience for attendees.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

2. Las Vegas Convention Center (LVCC)

 

  • Square Footage: 3.2 million sq. ft.

  • Highlights: One of the largest and most renowned convention centers in the world, LVCC features expansive exhibition halls, cutting-edge technology, and a central location close to the Strip. It’s ideal for large-scale events.

  • Fun Element: Incorporate augmented reality (AR) zones where attendees can explore virtual exhibits or compete in interactive games.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

3. Mandalay Bay Convention Center

 

  • Square Footage: Over 2 million sq. ft.

  • Highlights: With exhibition halls, ballrooms, and meeting spaces, Mandalay Bay offers incredible versatility and is conveniently located on the Strip. It’s a top choice for events of all sizes.

  • Fun Element: Create a tropical oasis with beach-themed lounges and live steel drum performances to match Mandalay Bay’s resort vibe. Luau cocktail hour, anyone?

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

4. Sands Expo & Convention Center (at The Venetian)

 

  • Square Footage: 1.2 million sq. ft.

  • Highlights: Located in the heart of the Strip, Sands Expo combines luxury and convenience. With direct access to The Venetian’s amenities, it’s perfect for industries looking to create an upscale experience.

  • Fun Element: Treat attendees to a Venetian-inspired gondola ride experience within a themed networking area. 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

5. Wynn and Encore Convention Center

 

  • Square Footage: 560,000 sq. ft.

  • Highlights: Known for elegance and attention to detail, Wynn and Encore offer a refined environment for luxury trade shows and events targeting high-end audiences.

  • Fun Element: Introduce a luxury spa corner with mini massages or wellness activities to keep attendees relaxed and engaged.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

6. ARIA Resort & Casino Convention Center

 

  • Square Footage: Over 500,000 sq. ft.

  • Highlights: ARIA’s modern design and premium amenities make it a standout choice for industries like tech, fashion, and entertainment. Its sleek spaces are as functional as they are stylish.

  • Fun Element: Offer interactive tech demos using ARIA’s advanced technology infrastructure, AI-powered lead generation and sales qualification software. This is a favorite of Rosie the Robot!

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

7.  Las Vegas World Market Center

 

  • Square Footage: Over 5 million sq. ft.

  • Highlights: Tailored for trade shows focused on home furnishings, design, and lifestyle products, this downtown venue offers vast showroom spaces and specialized facilities.

  • Fun Element: Add a design competition where attendees can vote on creative room setups or furniture displays.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

8.  Las Vegas Resorts World

 

  • Square Footage: Modern and customizable spaces.

  • Highlights: As one of the newest additions to the Strip, Resorts World offers innovative layouts and cutting-edge technology, making it ideal for forward-thinking industries.

  • Fun Element: Set up a futuristic lounge with holographic displays and robotic servers to reflect Resorts World’s modern aesthetic.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

9. T-Mobile Arena

 

  • Square Footage: Large-scale arena setting.

  • Highlights: Perfect for experiential marketing events and large trade shows, T-Mobile Arena provides a vibrant atmosphere and ample space for creativity.

  • Fun Element: Host live performances or esports tournaments on the arena stage to captivate large audiences.
