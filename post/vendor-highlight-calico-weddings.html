Weddings are often one of the most important days of our lives and here at MiHi Entertainment, we cherish our clients as well as our amazing vendors. The experience you have on this special day is extremely meaningful and we always hope to give you the best experience possible. We believe that wedding planning is an art that is approached with consideration of precision, aesthetics, and romance. That’s something that our friends at Calico believe as well.

 
 
 

Calico Weddings is an award-winning wedding planning team and serves the greater Front Range area. The natural inspiration behind Calico weddings comes from an intrinsic desire to celebrate and congratulate love. The Calico team values a commitment to excellent and beautiful work with a keen eye for details and design. With combined experience in corporate event planning and client services, their team is well equipped to guide your every step!

Calico was conceptualized and created in 2019 by Corporate Event Planner, Ashley Sirjord when she realized that her true passion for events was in creating beautiful ceremonies and providing excellent service for those looking to Tie the Knot. Ashley moved to Colorado from California and believes that Calico Weddings is where the warm Pacific breeze of the California coast meets the snow-capped Rocky Mountains of Colorado.

 

Calico Weddings has a passion for intimate events and their detail-oriented approach will make your special day a magical and forever memorable experience. They provide every service from full wedding planning to day-of coordination. Providing design inspiration, bridal checklists, budget tracking, and wedding day timelines, they have made a name for themselves, creating a multifaceted approach by true industry professionals.

 
 
 

Another attribute of great wedding planning is knowing that a “one size fits all” approach is not always the way to go and Ashley and her team are very aware of that fact. Whether you’re planning an intimate, destination wedding or a wedding for the masses they will be thrilled to assist you with your planning. In addition, they have worked with a great variety of couples and are ready and willing to work with you to plan every aspect of the big day or let you focus on other facets of your wedding while they take care of the rest! Calico understands that every couple and every personality is different and they are always willing to work together however you would like.

 
 
 

No matter how you would like to celebrate your wedding in the beautiful state of Colorado Calico will be sure to take care of your needs. From the gorgeous venues across the Front Range to the Peaks of the Rocky Mountains Calico will be there for you!
