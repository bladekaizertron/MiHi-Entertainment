At 127 years old, Elitch Gardens is one of the country’s premier amusement parks. These guys know how to have fun! It’s kinda their job. So, when they planned their annual employee appreciation party, they knew who to call.

 

Nope. Not the ghostbusters, but close guess.

 

If you guessed MiHi Photo Booth, voted Denver’s best photo booth service, then you deserve to treat yourself to an amazing ride on SlideZilla this summer!

 

We packed up our Classic Booth and headed to Grant-Humphreys mansion.

 

If you haven’t visited this iconic Denver location, you owe it to yourself to visit as soon as possible! Built for Colorado’s third governor and located in the Quality Hill neighborhood, this neoclassical mansion is the perfect setting for weddings, receptions, holiday parties, meetings, and retreats.

 

In other words, we’re no strangers there. It comes with the territory of being Denver’s go-to photo booth solution.

 

We wanted to do something special for the amazing employees of Elitch Gardens. We had an amazing time at their last employee appreciation party and wanted to return the love.

 

We brought our green screen. That way we could be compact, but give people the choice of six different holiday backgrounds. Everybody loves choices. Especially when every choice is awesome. Fortunately, we love it when people take lots of photos!

 

It was a smashing success!

 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 

One of our favorite parts of working employee appreciation parties is seeing everyone mingling and letting their hair down, so to speak. When you have a great crew of people, you work hard and play hard.

 

Elitch Gardens has been providing world class entertainment spanning three centuries. It’s inspiring to see the camaraderie between all of the employees. They provide forever memories with their rides and concerts. We provide our forever memories with our photos.

 

Having a photo booth at your party is a requirement at this point. Every good party planner knows this.

 

People love the relaxed atmosphere of our setup. Our friendly attendants know how to coax the best photos out of anyone. We have a get up for any occasion, from classy to edgy!

 

We live in a world of instant gratification. When you want it and you want it now. We do that. Get your photos in moments. Send your photos to your phones through SMS (a fancy way to say text). Post immediately on social media.

 

Boom. Bam. Done.

 

After the night wound down, we packed up our gear and headed out. We took a moment under the leafless trees to reflect on the history of the Grant-Humphreys mansion. It was another beautiful night in Denver, Colorado, which meant that it was another amazing night to be alive.
