Denver’s Best Wedding Venues: Say “I Do” in Style

Planning a wedding in Colorado? You’re in for a treat! The Centennial State is known for its breathtaking views, luxurious accommodations, and one-of-a-kind spaces that set the perfect stage for your big day. Whether you’re dreaming of an elegant celebration or a cozy gathering, Colorado offers venues to suit every couple’s style. Here are some of Denver’s top wedding ideas and venues—and a few gems just beyond the city.

 
 
 

1. Rooftop Weddings

 

Urban Elegance Meets Stunning Views

 
 
 

Dreaming of a chic ceremony with the Denver skyline as your backdrop? A rooftop wedding offers the ultimate in modern romance. Denver boasts numerous rooftop venues that pair sweeping views with city lights for unforgettable vibes. For couples looking to elevate their wedding entertainment, adding a 360 Photo Booth is the perfect way to capture stunning moments against the mountain backdrop. Consider spots like the Rooftop16 where you can view the Denver skyline and the gondola makes the perfect photo op. 

 
 
 

2. The Manor House

 

     A Historic Dream

 
 
 

Nestled in the foothills, The Manor House is a historic gem offering timeless elegance. Dating back to 1914, this venue combines classic interiors with breathtaking outdoor settings. It’s an ideal location for couples seeking a mix of intimacy and grandeur. Pair this venue with our Vintage Photo Booth to give guests a fun and interactive way to create memories.

 
 
 

3. Clocktower Events

 

     Whimsical and Intimate

 
 
 

For those who love unique spaces, Clocktower Events in downtown Denver offers a vintage vibe with stunning panoramic views. This intimate venue is perfect for smaller gatherings where every detail feels personal. Couples can elevate the fun by including top wedding ideas like a our mirror photo booth to capture the ambiance.

 
 
 

4. Gaylord Rockies Resort

 

     Luxury and Convenience

 
 
 

Located near Denver International Airport, the Gaylord Rockies Resort is perfect for couples with out-of-town guests. This venue combines convenience with luxury, featuring both indoor and outdoor options. To wow your guests, consider adding the glambot photo booth to the festivities—it’s our luxury product and wedding guest favorite for capturing the magic.

 
 
 

5. The Broadmoor

 

     Colorado Springs Sophistication

 
 
 

The Broadmoor is synonymous with luxury. Located in Colorado Springs, this world-class resort offers breathtaking views, elegant ballrooms, and exceptional service. Enhance the grandeur by incorporating top wedding entertainment options like our custom luxury photography set to keep guests engaged throughout the evening.

 
 
 

6. The Little Nell

 

     Aspen's Mountain Majesty

 
 
 

Aspen’s The Little Nell is the epitome of mountain elegance. With alpine views, world-class dining, and a luxurious ambiance, this five-star venue is perfect for any season. Incorporating our custom ski lift set into your reception allows guests to experience the mountain magic that reflects the natural beauty of the Rockies.

 
 
 

7. Spruce Mountain Ranch

 

     Rustic Charm for All Seasons

 
 
 

Embrace Colorado’s rustic charm at Spruce Mountain Ranch. This venue shines in any weather, offering cozy indoor spaces and picturesque outdoor settings. To make your day even more memorable, consider unique wedding entertainment options like our themed saloon photo booth set that fits the rustic aesthetic and provides your guests with a true western experience.

 
 
 

8. Wings Over the Rockies

 

     Bold and Unique

 
 
 

For couples seeking something adventurous, Wings Over the Rockies Air & Space Museum provides a bold and unconventional venue. Its massive hangar and historic aircraft create a modern, industrial-chic vibe. Adding the AI Photo Booth to this distinctive setting ensures your guests leave with memorable snapshots custom generated with your design from your special day. 

 
 
 

Final Thoughts

 

Colorado is a haven for couples seeking unique and breathtaking venues for their wedding. From elegant rooftops to luxurious resorts and rustic ranches, the state offers endless options to suit any style.
