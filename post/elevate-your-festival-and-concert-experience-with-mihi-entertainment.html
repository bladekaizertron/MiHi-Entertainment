M50 Roamer Booth

 

Capture the Fun Anywhere with High-Quality Photos

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

The M50 Roamer Booth is designed to bring the photo booth experience to your guests, no matter where they are at your festival or concert. This portable booth roams the event, allowing attendees to capture candid moments and share them instantly on social media. What sets our Roamer Booth apart is its use of a high-quality M50 camera, ensuring that every photo is crystal clear and vibrant.

 
 
 

• High-Quality Output: Unlike standard booths that use iPad cameras, our Roamer Booth uses an M50 camera, delivering stunning, high-resolution photos that capture every detail.

 
 
 

• Mobility: Our Roamer Booth can navigate through crowds, reaching guests who may not make it to a stationary photo booth.

 
 
 

• Instant Sharing: Attendees can instantly share their high-quality photos and GIFs on social media, spreading the excitement of your event far and wide.

 
 
 

• Engagement: The Roamer Booth is a fantastic way to keep guests engaged and entertained throughout the event.
