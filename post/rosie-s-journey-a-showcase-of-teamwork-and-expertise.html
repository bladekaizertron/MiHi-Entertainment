Rosie's Journey: A Showcase of Teamwork and Expertise

Administrator
•
December 15, 2025

Have you met Rosie, our innovative robot photo booth and a true standout when it comes to guest engagement? She recently had quite the adventure at the IMEX 2024 show, where MiHi Entertainment partnered with Destination Colorado at Mandalay Bay in Las Vegas.

 
 
 

Rosie was carefully packed and shipped, ready to take the spotlight. However, upon arrival, our team discovered that part of her head had suffered a crack during transit—a less-than-ideal situation, to say the least. But at MiHi Entertainment, we're no strangers to unexpected challenges. Our team immediately sprang into action with the professionalism and resourcefulness that comes from years of experience in the events industry.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

Our onsite operations team worked quickly to restore Rosie’s aesthetic appeal, ensuring she was as presentable as possible. Simultaneously, our office team went into overdrive to locate a backup. Fortunately, with a national network at our fingertips, thanks to our founder, Jeff Libby, we were able to make a few calls, securing a replacement Rosie in no time.

 
 
 

Meanwhile, our Director of Sales took to the streets of Las Vegas to give Rosie a personalized touch for her debut. In true Colorado style, she was outfitted with a cowgirl hat and a custom jean jacket emblazoned with the Destination Colorado logo, ensuring she made a memorable impression.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

This experience highlighted the strength and adaptability of our team. We’re proud of how everyone came together, handling the situation with professionalism and creativity to ensure Rosie—and Destination Colorado—shined at the event. In the events industry, challenges are inevitable, but what truly matters is how you respond. For us, it’s about quick thinking, teamwork, and always delivering exceptional results.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

Thank you to Destination Colorado for trusting us to represent your brand. After ten years in business, we continue to uphold the standard that no matter the obstacle, our team is ready to deliver solutions that allow our clients to shine.
