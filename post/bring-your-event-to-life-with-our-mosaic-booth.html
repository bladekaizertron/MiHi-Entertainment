Looking to add a unique and memorable touch to your next event? Our Mosaic Booth offers a stunning way to capture and showcase all those special moments. By assembling a large number of photos into a beautiful mosaic, we create a masterpiece that reflects the essence of your event.

 
 
 

Our Mosaic Booth takes individual photos taken from our booth and assembles them into a larger image, creating a striking mosaic-style masterpiece. It’s a fantastic way to showcase the collective joy and memories from your event in a single, stunning visual.

 
 
 

Perfect for corporate events, our Mosaic Booth fosters team unity through experiential elements that are both immersive and interactive. Whether you choose a physical setup at your event or a virtual experience, our Mosaic Booth brings your entire team together, creating shared memories and strengthening bonds. These interactive experiences encourage collaboration and engagement, making your corporate gathering not just an event, but a memorable experience that resonates with every participant.

 
 
 

Choose Your Mosaic Style

 

We offer three fantastic options to suit your event needs:

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

Physical Mosaic: Guests take photos at the booth, and we create a tangible mosaic at the event.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

Digital Mosaic:

 

All photos are compiled into a digital mosaic that can be displayed in real-time or shared online.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

Post-Event Printed Mosaic: Photos are taken during the event, and we assemble the mosaic afterward.

 
 
 

The final masterpiece is printed and shipped to you.

 
 
 

 
 
 

Experience the Magic Virtually

 

Can’t be at the booth physically? No problem! With our Virtual Mosaic Booth, guests can scan a QR code and take photos from their phones, no matter where they are. These photos are then added to the mosaic, making it a perfect solution for long-distance participation and inclusivity.

 
 
 

How It Works

 

Photo Taking Whether in-person or virtually, guests snap their photos.

 

Assembly Photos are collected and arranged to form the larger image.

 

Display The mosaic can be displayed digitally, created on-site, or printed post-event.
