Extreme Strings: A High-Energy
Live Electric Violin Performance Act

Extreme Strings is a live music experience featuring an electric violinist. Experience the energy and artistry of a high-energy performance that brings classical instruments into the modern era with stunning visual effects and dynamic sound.

WEDDINGS: A PERSONALIZED

For weddings, Extreme Strings provides tailored ceremony and cocktail hour packages, collaborating with couples to create a personalized musical timeline that reflects their personalities and preferences.

It details services from prelude music to processional/recessional songs, unity candle melodies, and upbeat cocktail hour tunes, emphasizing how the electric violinist adds elegance and energy.

A specific highlight mentions a performance at Sunspot Lodge in Winter Park Resort, featuring a romantic ceremony overlooking the valley and an energetic cocktail hour with popular songs like "Despacito."

During the reception, the violinist joined forces with a DJ from Elite Entertainment, captivating guests on the dance floor with a fusion of live violin and DJ beats, even performing a rousing rendition of 'The Devil Went Down to Georgia.'

CORPORATE EVENTS: FUSION OF LIVE

MUSIC AND DJ

For corporate events, Extreme Strings offers a unique "fusion" product, where the electric violinist performs alongside a DJ, creating a dynamic and immersive musical experience. This fusion style allows the violinist to improvise and complement the DJ's grooves, adding an extra layer of energy and excitement to the event.

One of their most memorable corporate events was Feel Famous 2024, where the violinist seamlessly transitioned from atmospheric, space-themed music to a lively dance party, complete with LED lights on the electric violin to match the event's neon-colored theme.
