Extreme Strings is a captivating live music experience featuring an electric violinist who performs a wide range of genres, from classical to modern top 40 hits, EDM, country, hip-hop, and even rap. Based in Denver, Colorado, with a team also operating in Milwaukee, Extreme Strings offers a unique and engaging entertainment option for weddings, corporate events, and social gatherings throughout Colorado and the Midwest.

 
 
 

WEDDINGS: A PERSONALIZED

 
 
 
 

For weddings, Extreme Strings provides tailored ceremony and cocktail hour packages, collaborating with couples to create a personalized musical timeline that reflects their personalities and preferences. From prelude music to processional and recessional songs, unity candle melodies, and upbeat cocktail hour tunes, the electric violinist adds a touch of elegance and energy to the celebration. One of their standout wedding performances took place at the Sunspot Lodge in Winter Park Resort, where the violinist played a romantic ceremony overlooking the valley, followed by an energetic cocktail hour featuring popular songs like “Despacito.”

 
 
 

During the reception, the violinist joined forces with a DJ from Elite Entertainment, captivating guests on the dance floor with a fusion of live violin and DJ beats, even performing a rousing rendition of “The Devil Went Down to Georgia.”

 
 
 

Corporate Events: Fusion of Live Music and DJ

 
 
 
 

For corporate events, Extreme Strings offers a unique “fusion” product, where the electric violinist performs alongside a DJ, creating a dynamic and immersive musical experience. This fusion style allows the violinist to improvise and complement the DJ's grooves, adding an extra layer of energy and excitement to the event. One of their most memorable corporate events was Feel Famous 2024, where the violinist seamlessly transitioned from atmospheric, space-themed music to a lively dance party, complete with LED lights on the electric violin to match the event's neon-colored theme.

 
 
 

Collaboration with DJs and Referrals

 
 
 

While Extreme Strings focuses solely on providing live music, they have established strong relationships with numerous DJs in the area. If a client requires both a violinist and a DJ, Extreme Strings can provide trusted referrals and ensure a seamless collaboration between the two performers, creating a cohesive and unforgettable event experience. With their versatility, professionalism, and ability to captivate audiences, Extreme Strings offers a unique and engaging live music experience that elevates any event to new heights of excitement and entertainment.
