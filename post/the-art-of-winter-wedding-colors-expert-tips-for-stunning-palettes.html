Planning a Winter Wedding?

 

Winter weddings are magical, filled with the warmth of celebration and the beauty of the season. Whether you’re envisioning a cozy lodge or a glamorous winter wonderland, choosing the right color palette is key to creating the perfect atmosphere for your special day.

 
 
 

Here are our expert tips for stunning winter holiday wedding colors and three breathtaking palettes to inspire you.

 
 
 
 
 

PALETTE 1: BLUE SPRUCE & SNOW

 

Frost White, Pale Mint, Blue Spruce, Misty Blue, Deep Ocean Blue

 
 

This palette combines cool, serene shades of blue and green with the crispness of white. It’s perfect for an outdoor winter wedding or a venue with natural elements, evoking the tranquility of snow-covered trees and frosty skies.

PALETTE 2: GILDED SAPPHIRE

 

Midnight Blue, Dusty Sky, Soft Champagne, Gold Leaf, Antique Bronze

 
 

This luxurious blend of deep golds and blues brings sophistication and warmth to any winter wedding. The rich metallic hues paired with cool sapphire tones create a stunning contrast, ideal for formal indoor venues or glamorous evening ceremonies.

PALETTE 3: CRIMSON & PINE

 

Soft Ivory, Pine Green, Blush Rose, Light Crimson, Deep Crimson

 
 

This palette features classic shades of red and green, elevated with soft ivory and blush tones to create a romantic and festive feel. The mix of bold crimson and deep green balances perfectly, offering a refined take on traditional holiday colors.
