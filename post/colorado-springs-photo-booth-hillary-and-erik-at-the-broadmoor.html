Colorado Springs Photo Booth Rentals

 

Our team here at MiHi Entertainment has always felt a responsibility to create a photo booth experience as specific to our clients as possible. This is only possible when we are able to become a part of the larger intention and design of an event as a whole. The Hillary and Erik wedding was one of the best examples of a day when all the elements of a marvelous wedding came together to make an event gorgeous, luxurious, and downright fun.

 
 
 

Black and White Photo Booth Filter

 

We knew our couple had the right idea when picking MiHi Entertainment as well as the beautiful Broadmoor Hotel. The couple was also quite savvy in their choice of the Corporate booth as well as the black and white filter that creates images that are simple but elegant and reflect the polished ambiance of the grand and ornate venue. Not only was the couple obviously well versed in the sophistication of their perfect day but was also clearly concerned with ensuring the laughter and festivities ensued. This is clearly evident from the dazzling photos full of smiles and jubilation. Once the preceding were over our wedding party was able to enjoy the opulent and exciting property that is the Broadmoor Hotel.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 

The Broadmoor Hotel in Colorado Springs

 

Nestled in a quiet nook of Colorado Springs, The Broadmoor Hotel is one of Colorado’s most luxurious and breathtaking venues and vacation destinations. The 5-Diamond resort offers some of the most compelling amenities and magnificent views that any extravagant lodging has to offer. The couple was very interested in maintaining a European styled celebration that would reflect Erik’s Italian heritage while still remaining close to friends and family. The Broadmoor offers an ambiance that is very European inspired and was an exceptional choice for Hillary as well as her family has long frequented the palatial hotel.

 

Gaining inspiration from the colors and atmosphere, Hillary and Erik alongside Sarah Viera of Sarah Viera Events created soft palates, curated flatware, dusty blue invitations, and harmonious flower arrangements that would be reminiscent of a Parisian foray. Hillary also added to the old world vibe by adorning a subtle gold dress that went perfectly with the fantastic hair and make-up work of Megan Cary and her associates of Megan Cary Artistry. “Hillary and her ladies were all a delight and VERY easy to work with. The venue staff and Sarah Viera did an amazing job at ensuring everything went perfectly as planned which in turn helps nail the Hair & Makeup looks in a timely and organized fashion. At the end, all the ladies looked absolutely stunning and Carrie King photography captured the ladies’ beauty and our artistry amazingly! We are very honored to be part of such a talented vendor group for her day!”

 

At the end of the day, not only were Erik and Hillary very precise and artistic in their choices for such a charming wedding but they also made great choices in the vendors that attended. Our MiHi Entertainment team was delighted to be a part of this upbeat day and enjoyed capturing the elegant yet jovial photos of the wedding party. It was great to be a part of such a great team of vendors that helped put together such a perfect day.
