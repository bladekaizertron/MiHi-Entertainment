Beauty and History

 
 
 

The Addison, a vision of Addison Mizner and was built in 1926. Set near the beaches of Palm Beach County, sporting historic architecture, this venue has it all. If your planning your big day close to home, or it is going to be a destination wedding, you can’t go wrong with this choice.

Big or Small-The Addison has it All

 
 
 

From the outdoor courtyard that is picturesquely surrounded by banyan trees, to the indoor event space options you can find the perfect area for you and your guests. The main dining room is reminiscent of the 20’s, making it an ideal sot for a themed wedding. The space with its beautiful wrought iron chandeliers, the cypress accents, and the time period motif, make you feel like the luxury is all about you. If your looking for something just as beautiful with a more relaxed feel, the Mizner ballroom may be your match. The neutral colored wood floors, with stunning cypress ceilings and gorgeous barn doors are perfect to match our wedding colors.
