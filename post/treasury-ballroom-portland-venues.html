That little play on words, is very powerful. Built in 1917, this historic bank building is now a gorgeous venue just waiting for you to celebrate your big day with it. If your first though was, a bank? Really? Well, this venue is unlike any you have seen before. Just imagine as you and your guests arrive to see the amazing roman architecture, the Corinthian columns towering before the entrance. Treasury Ballroom is really a sight to behold.

 
 
 

A Portland photo booth is ideal at this venue! So many lovely backdrops to choose from. Each with its own personality to it. Make each photo taken a little more special as it will not only capture you and your guests, but the stunning location that you chose for the event.

You and your guests will feel like you have stepped into a different world.
