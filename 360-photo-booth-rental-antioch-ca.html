What is a 360 Photo Booth?

A 360 photo booth is an innovative entertainment experience that captures guests from every angle. Unlike traditional photo booths that take static images, our 360 booth features a rotating camera arm that circles around your guests while they pose on a platform, creating cinematic slow-motion videos that look straight out of a music video or red carpet premiere.

The technology behind MIHI Photo Booth's 360 experience includes:

  • High-Speed Camera Rotation — Professional-grade cameras mounted on a precision arm capture smooth, dynamic footage as they orbit around guests in 2-3 seconds.
  • Instant Social Sharing — Videos are processed on-site and delivered immediately via text, email, or QR code, allowing guests to share their moment on Instagram, TikTok, and other platforms before they even leave your event.
  • Customizable Overlays & Branding — Every video can feature your event logo, custom graphics, music, and digital effects that match your theme, from elegant wedding monograms to corporate branding for product launches.
