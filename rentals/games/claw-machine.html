What is a Claw Machine?

A claw machine is a classic arcade game that's all about skill, luck, and excitement. Guests use a joystick to control a mechanical claw, trying to grab a prize from inside the machine and drop it into the winning slot. It's a fun, interactive experience that keeps people coming back for more while creating a lively and playful atmosphere at any event.

Using our claw machines is simple and stress-free. We handle the setup, so all you need to do is stock it with your chosen prizes and let your guests take turns testing their luck and skill. Whether you're encouraging friendly competition, rewarding attendees, or adding a nostalgic touch to your event, our claw machine rentals are guaranteed to bring excitement and engagement to any occasion.

Frequently asked questions

Have questions about our Claw Machine Rentals? We're happy to answer them!

  • How much space does the claw machine need?

    • Our claw machines are compact and fit comfortably in most event spaces. We recommend a space of about 4x4 feet for easy access and gameplay.

  • Can the claw machine be branded?

    • Yes! We can wrap the machine with your logo, colors, or custom design, making it a perfect fit for brand activations and corporate events.

  • What types of prizes can go inside?

    • You can fill the machine with small items like keychains, sunglasses, toys, gift cards, or branded giveaways. We’ll help you choose prizes that fit your event theme.

  • Do you provide the prizes?

    • Prizes can be supplied by you, or we can help source items based on your budget and event needs.

  • How do guests use the claw machine?

    • It’s simple! Guests use the joystick to move the claw, then press the button to try and grab a prize. The challenge keeps them coming back for more.

  • Is setup and delivery included?

    • Yes, our team handles delivery, setup, and removal, so all you need to do is enjoy the fun with your guests.
